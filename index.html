    Initiative

    First author, advised by Dr. Myungin Lee

    Deep learning for plankton estimation from satellite imagery

    Shipboard instruments measure plankton one point at a time, while NASA's PACE satellite observes the entire ocean surface. I develop compact deep learning models that estimate plankton from hyperspectral satellite data when paired training examples are scarce.

    The change I made
    The project began as visualization work on NASA's PACE data. Because I already had independent research experience, I was encouraged to branch off, and I redirected it toward deep learning: estimating plankton directly from the satellite imagery.
    My role
    Project lead and first author. I designed and built the models, and grew the work from an independent study into a team project.
    Approach
    Compact convolutional models evaluated under a strict grouped protocol, so near-duplicate data never appears in both training and testing.
    Timeline
    January 2026 to present

    First-author manuscript currently under review.

    Representation learning for protein structure

    I study whether deep representations of protein structure generalize to protein families never seen during training, a central challenge for detecting distant evolutionary relationships between proteins.

  • Creative direction

    Project lead, with collaborator Abdul Rahem

    Memento of Self

    An interactive installation about the gap between a fluid, ever-changing self and its fixed digital capture. A multi-camera booth photographs each visitor, custom software reconstructs them as a painterly, dissolving 3D portrait, and they leave with a printed copy.

    The creative decision
    Rather than aiming for a faithful 3D replica, I chose to render each visitor as a painterly, dissolving portrait, so the reconstruction itself expresses the idea that a person can't be fixed in a single capture. Confirm or rewrite in your own words.
    Research question
    Does appearance-based (photometric) reconstruction degrade more gracefully than geometric accuracy when only a few camera views are available? An Azure Kinect depth camera provides independent geometric ground truth.
    My role
    I built the sparse-view Gaussian Splatting reconstruction pipeline, designed and fabricated the booth (housing, camera mounts, lighting, and trigger systems), and integrated live rendering in Unreal Engine for exhibition.
    Tools
    Gaussian Splatting, Azure Kinect, Unreal Engine, CNC fabrication, physical computing
    Timeline
    June 2026 to present

    In progress. Documentation coming soon.
