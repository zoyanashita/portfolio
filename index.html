About

I'm completing a B.S. in Computer Science and a B.A. in Immersive Media Design at the University of Maryland. My research spans computer vision, computational biology, HCI, and deep learning, and I'm currently exploring computational imaging and graphics.

My immersive media work lets me test those ideas where art and technology meet. After my undergraduate degree, I plan to pursue a PhD in a computer vision field.
